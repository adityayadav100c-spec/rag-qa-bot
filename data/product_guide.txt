Fictional sample: Northstar Travel Mug

The Northstar Travel Mug holds 450 milliliters and is made from stainless steel.
It keeps drinks warm for up to four hours when the lid is closed. The mug is
hand-wash only and should not be placed in a microwave or dishwasher.

Fictional sample: Northstar Daypack

The Northstar Daypack has a 20-liter capacity, a padded laptop sleeve that fits
laptops up to 14 inches, and a water-resistant outer fabric. It is not fully
waterproof. The empty pack weighs 620 grams.

Fictional sample: Shipping and returns

Orders ship within two business days. Standard delivery usually takes three to
five business days. Unused items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged.
