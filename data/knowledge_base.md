# Northstar Demo Help Center

All names, policies, products, service levels, and numbers in this handbook are
invented for software demonstration. They are not a real merchant policy.

## Product: Northstar Travel Mug

The Travel Mug has a 450 ml capacity and a double-wall stainless-steel body.
When its lid is closed, it is designed to keep a hot drink warm for up to four
hours. Temperature retention depends on the starting drink temperature and
ambient conditions. The mug is hand-wash only. Do not put it in a dishwasher,
microwave, freezer, or on a stove. Wash the lid separately and allow it to dry
before storing. The mug is not guaranteed to be leakproof when placed on its
side.

## Product: Northstar Daypack

The Daypack has a 20 litre capacity and an empty weight of 620 grams. A padded
sleeve fits laptops up to 14 inches measured diagonally. Its outer fabric is
water resistant but the bag is not waterproof; use a separate rain cover for
heavy rain. Do not machine wash the pack. Spot-clean with a damp cloth and mild
soap, then air dry. The laptop sleeve is not a substitute for a hard case.

## Product: Northstar Desk Lamp

The Desk Lamp uses a replaceable 5 W LED bulb with an E26 base. The lamp has
three brightness levels selected by the touch control on its base. The product
box includes the lamp and a power adapter. It does not include a spare bulb.
Unplug the adapter before cleaning. The lamp is for indoor use on a stable,
level surface.

## Product: Northstar Wireless Headphones

The Wireless Headphones connect over Bluetooth and include a USB-C charging
cable. A full charge typically supports up to 30 hours of playback at moderate
volume. Battery life varies with volume, codec, and connection quality. To
enter pairing mode, hold the power button for three seconds while the headset
is off. The microphone is intended for ordinary calls and is not a studio
recording microphone.

## Product care and warranty

Keep electronics away from liquids and extreme heat. Stop using a device if
its battery swells, becomes unusually hot, or smells unusual; contact support
instead of attempting a repair. The fictional demo warranty covers manufacturing
defects for one year from the order date. It does not cover accidental damage,
normal wear, unauthorized repair, or use contrary to the care instructions.
Proof of purchase is required to request warranty service.

## Ordering and payment

An order confirmation is sent after checkout. A pending card authorization is
not the same as a captured payment. Orders can only be changed or cancelled
while their status is "processing"; once handed to the carrier, the customer
must follow the returns process. The demo store supports major credit cards.
Never send full card numbers or security codes to support.

## Shipping

Demo orders are prepared within two business days. Standard delivery is
estimated at three to five business days after dispatch. These estimates are
not guarantees and do not include holidays or exceptional carrier delays.
Tracking details are sent when a carrier label is created; tracking scans may
take up to 24 hours to appear. A shipping estimate cannot be used to infer the
current location of an individual parcel.

## Returns and refunds

Unused products may be returned within 30 days of delivery in their original
condition. The customer pays return postage unless the item arrived damaged or
the wrong item was sent. Contact support for a return authorization before
shipping an item back. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after
the returned item has been inspected. Processing time at the payment provider
varies. Items marked final sale and personalized items are not returnable.

## Damaged or incorrect deliveries

If an item arrives damaged or is not the item ordered, keep the packaging and
contact support within seven days of delivery with the order number and clear
photos of the product and packaging. Do not dispose of the item before support
responds. Support will explain whether a replacement, return, or refund is
appropriate. Do not include payment-card details in an email.

## Account and privacy

Use the account recovery link on the sign-in screen if you cannot access an
account. Support will never ask for a password or one-time login code. This
demo application should only be tested with fictional data. Do not upload
personal records, credentials, payment data, or confidential business
documents to a public or shared deployment.

## Troubleshooting: headphones

If the headphones do not appear in Bluetooth settings, turn them off, forget
the old pairing on the device, and hold the power button for three seconds to
enter pairing mode. Charge them for at least 20 minutes before retrying if the
status light does not turn on. Try one source device at a time. If audio drops,
move the source device closer and temporarily disconnect other paired devices.
These steps do not diagnose battery or hardware failure.

## Troubleshooting: desk lamp

If the lamp does not turn on, check the adapter connection and try a known-good
outlet. Use the included adapter and the specified bulb type only. Unplug the
lamp before checking or replacing a bulb. Stop if the cable is damaged, the
adapter is hot, or there is a burning smell; contact support rather than
opening the electrical housing.

## Contacting support

Include the order number, product name, a short description of the issue, and
the steps already tried. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or
unrelated personal documents. Support hours and contact channels are
intentionally not specified in this demo, so the application must not invent
them.

## Questions this knowledge base cannot answer

This handbook contains no real-time inventory, order status, tracking events,
current prices, coupons, legal advice, medical advice, store locations, live
opening hours, or actual support contact details. When asked for these, the
assistant should say the information is unavailable in the supplied sources
and direct the user to verify through an official channel.
